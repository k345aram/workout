Training log
============

Files
- index.html            the app (open this file)
- manifest.webmanifest  app name and icon info for "Add to Home screen"
- icons/                the running stickman icon (192, 512 and iPhone sizes)

How to use
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Open index.html in your phone or computer browser.
3. To keep it on your phone, upload the folder to any web host and use
   "Add to Home screen" from the browser menu.

Notes
- Your entries are saved in the browser you open the app in. If you open it
  from a different browser, device, or address, you will start with an empty log.
- The fonts in the Theme menu load from the internet. Without a connection
  the app falls back to your phone's default font.
